Help Center — FAQ

Everything you need to know about Ava's Digital Trading Card.

  • This is Ava's Digital Trading Card — a personal, interactive website designed as a collectible trading card. It showcases Ava's identity, stats, and special abilities in the style of a Water-type Pokémon-inspired card, built entirely with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

    Think of it as a next-level personal portfolio card that you can flip, battle with, and explore!

  • On desktop: simply hover your mouse cursor over the card. The card will smoothly rotate 180° to reveal its back face, powered by a CSS 3D transform.

    On mobile: tap anywhere on the card to toggle between the front and back. Tap again to flip it back. The card remembers its orientation until you tap again!

  • The ⚔️ Battle! button launches the Battle Arena — a fun dice-roll mini-game! When clicked, Ava faces off against a randomly selected opponent (like a "Bug in Production 🐛" or "CSS Centering Boss 📐").

    Both Ava and the opponent roll a 20-sided die. Ava's score = her Drizzy Drake stat (94) + dice roll. The higher total score wins. Results are displayed in big letters: either AVA WINS! 🏆 or REMATCH NEEDED! 💥. Keep battling for glory!

  • Irresistible is Ava's signature Special Move, displayed on both the front and back of the card. It reads: "Radiates effortless charm and magnetic energy. Stuns opposing cards and leaves everyone completely captivated."

    On the back of the card, you can actively trigger it by pressing the 🌊 Activate Irresistible Ability button. The card glows with a water-blue aura, and a status banner confirms the ability is live. There's a 2.5-second cooldown before you can cast it again!

  • This card is Ava's personal digital trading card, tailored to represent her uniquely. It is not a template or generator available to the public — but the good news is that it was hand-directed by Ava and built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, so anyone with web skills could adapt the concept.

    If you're a developer and curious about the code, feel free to inspect the page source for a look at how the 3D flip, accordion animations, and battle system are built!

  • These are Ava's three fun, made-up stats that appear on the front of the card — inspired by trading card stat systems but playfully personalized:

    🍿 Snack Power (78/100) — Measures Ava's ability to sustain long coding sessions with strategic snack selection.

    😴 Nap Quality (63/100) — Tracks rest efficiency. High nap quality = faster bug fixing the next morning.

    🎵 Drizzy Drake (94/100) — Ava's certified Drake listener rating. Also happens to be her top battle stat — it's that powerful.

  • Ava chose the Water type because water is fluid, adaptive, and endlessly powerful — traits that resonate with her approach to design and engineering. Water flows around obstacles and finds creative paths forward, just like great problem-solving.

    Also, the oceanic deep-blue aesthetic is simply stunning — and Ava has strong opinions about color palettes. 💧

  • This site was hand-directed by Ava and built with AI assistance as a project for CSEN 163 — Web Design & Engineering at Santa Clara University.

    Every design decision — the oceanic Water-type theme, the Futura typography, the stats, the battle mechanics, and the Irresistible ability — was conceived and directed by Ava. The code was crafted collaboratively with an AI pair programmer.
